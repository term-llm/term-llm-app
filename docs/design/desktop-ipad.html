We have a free Saturday in Kyoto. Can you suggest a slower day, with a good walk and somewhere to stop for lunch?

Finch 10:39

I’d keep the day on the eastern side of the city, and leave plenty of room to wander.

  • Start at the Philosopher’s Path. Go early, while the canal is quiet. The walk is gentle; allow an hour rather than rushing it.
  • Take the long way to Nanzen-ji. Follow the small streets south and spend a little time around the brick aqueduct.
  • Make lunch the only fixed point. Look for a small soba shop near Okazaki, then decide whether you feel like a garden or a café.

That’s enough of a plan. You’ll see more by choosing one neighbourhood well than by crossing the city all day.

If it rains, swap the canal walk for a museum in Okazaki and keep the same unhurried lunch.

Read the design notes

More room. Less chrome.

A desktop & iPad design concept, not an implemented or live app. All people, agents and messages are fictional.

  • One 52px header. Native on Agents; agent-owned web header on Chat. Never both.
  • Comfortable reading. 17px chat prose, a 760px reading measure, 15px sidebar navigation. No scaled-down phone layout.
  • A persistent workspace. Switching destinations hides, rather than reconstructs, this chat. Draft and scroll survive within this page.
  • Sidebar, not a stretched tab bar. 220px on Mac, 200px on iPad. Below 901px it opens as a dismissible overlay.
  • Upstream boundary. The real web UI owns Chat’s header, typography and composer. These changes require upstream work; native must not duplicate or rewrite that header.
